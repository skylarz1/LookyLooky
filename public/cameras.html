FFmpeg Memory
Stops every FFmpeg, drops every viewer and starts the camera system fresh, without restarting the LookyLooky server. Panes reconnect on their own within a few seconds.
WebCodecs (Dashboard)
H.264/HEVC video + Opus audio, decoded in the browser.
GPUs expose only ~4–8 hardware decode slots. Panes over this budget (ranked: focused pane first, then rendered size) render as JPEG thumbnails, which use none. Set to 1 for "only the focused pane is full motion".
Viewers can still pick per pane (the HQ/LQ button on hover) or per layout (Quality in the toolbar).
MJPEG (HTTP endpoint)
/stream/:id/mjpeg + /stream/:id/snapshot for third-party consumers — also feeds the dashboard's thumbnail panes (over the full-motion decode budget).
Pipelines
A warm pipeline keeps the camera connection open with nobody watching, so a pane shows a picture the moment it opens. It costs one camera connection and a little network per warm stream.
